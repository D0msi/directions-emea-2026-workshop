# Should a free-text 'Customer Name' field be classified as personal data, even when most customers are companies?

**Side A:** Yes. Some customers are people and you can't tell which. Classify for the worst case.

**Side B:** No. Classifying everything as personal data makes the classification meaningless and slows down data-subject requests.
